      How to read it

      The two horizontal rails are shared power and ground. Every module gets power from the rails, while the ESP32 handles the signal wires and alert outputs.

      Build notes

      Use 3.3 V-safe modules. The LED needs its 220 Ω resistor, and the LDR is shown as a voltage divider into A0.

      Smart stick overview

      The smart stick is designed as a full assistive handle from top to bottom. The upper grip contains the SOS and control interface, the middle body holds the sensing and alert hardware, and the bottom section includes the tip, strap, and stability elements that help the user grip and navigate safely.

      Length1.1 m

      Full smart stick anatomy

      The stick is modeled as one complete assistive body, not just as a controller board. Every major part is placed to make the user understand the real handle, grip, alert modules, and emergency controls across the entire structure.

      SOS button

      The emergency SOS button sits near the upper handle for easy access. When pressed, it triggers the alert flow and sends the user’s location to the contact in an emergency event.

      Alert buzzer

      The buzzer is placed inside the upper side body to produce a loud warning when a fall or critical state is detected. It is visible in the side and top sections of the model.

      LDR sensor

      The light-dependent resistor senses ambient brightness and helps the stick decide when night mode or low-light LED assistance should activate.

      LED indicator

      The LED safely indicates ambient mode or warning state. It glows when the surrounding light is too low or an alert condition is active.

      GPS module

      The GPS receiver is positioned at the lower side area to track the location of the stick in real time and help send safe coordinates during emergencies.

      Cancel button

      The cancel button acts as the user-facing reset control. It can stop the emergency SMS or silence the alert when the user confirms the situation is safe.

      Grip strap

      The flexible strap around the handle helps secure the stick in the hand and improves comfort, stability, and grip while walking or during sudden motion.
